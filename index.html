Feature specs

54 features in 12 epics, one chapter per epic. Each chapter starts with the epic overview and per-phase deep dive, then one section per feature with requirements, logic, flow, edge cases and acceptance criteria. See all features in one table.

Chapter 14 · E01 · Phase 1, 2 · 7 featuresIdentity & AccessEvery person signs in with one identity, is linked to their league membership, and can only do what their role allowsChapter 15 · E02 · Phase 1, 2 · 6 featuresLeague Data FoundationThe league's data, rules and API exist in league-be, so every screen has something real to callChapter 16 · E03 · Phase 1, 2 · 3 featuresSchedule & Night ViewAnyone can find a fixture and see the state of a night. Captains see what to do nextChapter 17 · E04 · Phase 1, 2 · 4 featuresNight Operations: Put-up, Result, SubmitA captain runs all 5 matches of a night on a phone: put up players within the 23 cap, enter each result, submitChapter 18 · E05 · Phase 1, 2 · 6 featuresRoster & Player AdminCaptains keep rosters filled with paid members. Admins find players, change skill levels with a reason, and move players between teams, all traceableChapter 19 · E06 · Phase 1, 2 · 10 featuresPlatform & DeliveryThe app installs like a native app, works in English and Vietnamese, ships safely on a tag, and is observable and tested end to endChapter 20 · E07 · Phase 2 · 5 featuresLive ScoringReplace "type the final score" with shot-by-shot scoring on one phone per table: coin flip, lag, every turn, every rack, accolades. It works offline, and everyone else watches liveChapter 21 · E08 · Phase 2 · 1 featureScore Verification & DisputesBoth teams agree on the night's record before it counts. Agreement earns a standings bonus; disagreement becomes a dispute that an admin resolves with an audit trailChapter 22 · E09 · Phase 2 · 2 features9-Ball & HandicapRun 9-Ball divisions with point-based scoring and the 20-point split, and keep every player's skill level honest with data-driven suggestionsChapter 23 · E10 · Phase 2 · 2 featuresProfile & StandingsPlayers see their own record and achievements; everyone, signed in or not, sees division standings and resultsChapter 24 · E11 · Phase 2 · 2 featuresPayments & NotificationsCollect membership and weekly fees through the wallets Vietnamese players already use, and reach players where they already are (Zalo)Chapter 25 · E12 · Phase 3 · 8 featuresGrowthGrow from one league to many: more formats and divisions, tournaments, venue and sponsor value, and protection of the handicap system
